I am Jing Li. I received my Ph.D. in Educational Psychology from the University of Georgia. My research focuses on psychometrics, Rasch measurement, explanatory item response models, educational measurement, and survey methodology.

My work applies statistical and measurement models to examine survey validity, item difficulty, differential item functioning, item difficulty modeling, and response behavior in educational, psychological, and public health contexts.

I am particularly interested in using measurement models to better understand how item features, person characteristics, and response processes shape assessment and survey outcomes. My recent work includes studies on food insecurity measurement, survey misreporting, explanatory item response models, and educational assessment item difficulty.
